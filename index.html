How this data was made

  1. Screen. Human embryonic stem cells carrying a CRISPRi library (~13,000 guides against transcription factors and chromatin regulators, several guides per cell — high MOI) were profiled by single-cell RNA-seq in two states: definitive-endoderm differentiated (DE) and pluripotent (ES).
  2. Per-guide regression. Within each state, every gene's normalized expression is regressed on the cell × guide design matrix (ordinary least squares). Each guide–gene pair gets a coefficient: how much that guide shifts the gene's expression in the cells that carry it. A blank point means the pair was masked in the regression.
  3. Within-guide gene rank. For each guide, its ~10,000 gene coefficients are sorted; a gene's rank is the number of genes with a larger coefficient (0 = that guide's most-upregulated gene). Because rank is computed inside each guide's own profile, guides with large global effects don't dominate.
  4. The plot. Pick a gene: each point is one guide — x is its coefficient on that gene, y is the gene's rank within that guide (top = most upregulated). Top-right guides upregulate the gene strongly and specifically; bottom-left the reverse. Violet marks guides targeting the viewed gene itself; the active filter keeps only guides with validated on-target knockdown.

Genes offered in the picker are those with ≥ 15 guides significant at FDR < 0.05, plus a curated set; all guides are always shown. Coefficients are stored to 3 significant digits.
